Privacy notice

Effective July 27, 2026

We use first-party analytics to understand account registrations and feature usage. We do not use advertising pixels or sell/share this data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

What analytics collects

Your account identifier, registration and verification status, page views, clicks on named product features, extraction success or failure, and timestamps. Administrators can associate these events with your account to support and improve the service.

What analytics does not collect

Clinical-note text, diagnoses, extracted medical codes, microphone audio, IP addresses, precise location, browser fingerprints, or advertising identifiers. Never enter information into moBILLity unless you are authorized to process it.

Purpose, retention, and disclosure

We use analytics only for security, service operation, support, adoption measurement, and product improvement. Event-level analytics is retained for up to {{RETENTION_DAYS}} days. Account records remain while the account is active or as legally required. Analytics is not disclosed to ad networks or data brokers.

Your choices and rights

You may turn off and erase event analytics below while signed in. Depending on where you live, you may also request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of personal data, and appeal a denied request. We will verify requests before acting. We do not discriminate for exercising privacy rights.

Health and sensitive information

This analytics system is intentionally separated from clinical content. Whether HIPAA, the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule, state consumer-health laws, or other rules apply depends on how your organization uses and deploys the service. Appropriate contracts, access controls, risk assessments, incident response, and breach-notification procedures may still be required.
